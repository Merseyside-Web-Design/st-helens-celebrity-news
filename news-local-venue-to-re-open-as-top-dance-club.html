Local venue to re-open as top dance club

NEWS10 September 2002

Since the demise of Liverpool's premier nightclubs Cream and Garlands, the UK's top superstar DJs are now looking for a suitable North West venue to exploit with their excessive appearance fees. Local clubbers are said to be 'gutted' by the lack of suitable clubs in which to get off their faces and are desperate for a new fix.

Well thanks to St Helens Celebrity News, a favourite 80s 'copping off' disco-type place is to re-open with a line up to make the whole of Ibiza green with envy.

Yes, the old Sunday nights will be recaptured with a purposely refurbished clubhouse, featuring three rooms with different styles of music.

Police are said to be in favour of the new venture and will be actively encouraging drug usage in order to prevent trouble.

The quality of the line up is superb and the opening night has now been booked.

Main Room

Tony Blackburn, Paul Oakenfold, Judge Jules, John Carter, Fergie, Timo Maas

Bar

Bruno Brookes, Sacha, Seb Fontaine, Pete Tong, Lotty, X-Press 2

Bogs

Gary Ousalis, Justin Robertson, Armand Van Helden, Dave Clark, Chad Jackson & others

U2 have also been provisionally booked to play 'Red Rocks'.
