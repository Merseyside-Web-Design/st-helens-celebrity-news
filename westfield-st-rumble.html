Westfield St Rumble

It's Saturday night in St Helens town centre and there are four fights between you and a taxi home: the bouncer on the door, the Scouser who wants you to calm down, the dealer round the back of the taxi rank and, worst of all, the hen party. Best of three rounds each. Land enough hits to fill your pie meter, then lob a meat and potato at them.
