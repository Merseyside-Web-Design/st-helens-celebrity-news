The 'Tellins Dictionary

Can't understand a word they're saying? Here's your guide to speaking proper St Helens.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

A

Aarf-Knacker

Word used for large house brick. Good for 'lobbin' (see also L) at
green'ouse winders - submitted by Jane W

Ave

As in 'Its avin it down' - its raining heavily

B

Barley

Apparently, a spoonerism of 'parley', meaning truce - used in kids games as an excuse for not being caught.

Bortered

As in "He was bortered in mud." or even clorped.

Bull Bitter

Half a bitter in a pint glass an a bottle of bulls eye brown ale. Why? Who knows?

Burgie water

The black liquid that came off them when it rained. It's been a bit of a saying for years for a bad pint..."looks like fuckin' burgie
water".

C

Chincoff

A "Parrism" from the 1950's. Meaning - a bad cold caught from various sources ie. sitting on cold doorsteps, drinking out of damp glasses etc.

Chinny

As in being slightly annoyed or wound up by ones friend
e.g.OOHH look at 'im, he's got chinny coz you et 'is pie!

Clempt

"one o me favourite t'ellins words has to be clempt (possibly klempt) meaning starvin'... as in "savers on yer pastie, ah'm clempt""

Corporation Pop

Water

F

Faffin'

Messing, as in 'Stop faffin' about' contributed by Sean

Father or favors

It means a person looks like someone else, e.g. Dosen't he favor Kevin
Keegan....

Fettled

Fixed, sorted out

G

Galoshers

Those black pump things that kids wear at school.

Geritetten

'Please finish your meal', possibly used in the context of 'Worart onobehtattall its brew tarm, lerrus gerris answashed and get snap tins and geritetten'.

Gerroff

Telling someone to get off ...or implying that there kidding you

Grand National

Basically racing over everybody's hedges up the street - could be quite painful if you banged a knee on a hidden concrete post. Best played in Windleshaw Road.

H

Hanrags

Word of unclear meaning - sometimes used to call Saints when they were losing - more info needed

K

Keks

Trousers - as used in the phrase 'the bastard spilt his pint all over me keks' - suggested by Big Ant

Klempt

"one o me favourite t'ellins words has to be Klempt (possibly clempt) meaning starvin'... as in "savers on yer pastie, ah'm clempt""

L

Lobbin'

Word used instead of throwing. Not to be confused with 'lobby' (see L). - submitted by Jane W

Lobby

Hotpot without the crust. Ususally consisting of meat, (also substituted
with mince if between Giros) potatoes, turnip & carrot. - Jane W

M

Maud

Grilfriend - as in "Gooin arrt wiff ar maud"

Melt

As in 'Yer soft melt' contributed by Sean

me - used grammatically incorrectly

Still in use today "me" at the end of a sentence. e.g. - "ah proper fanceh an uxley's puddin me". Or "Aahm sick o' bein shagged rownd t'back o' Cindy's me. E sed e luvved meh."

Mither

(my-ther) - Fo be bothered by an idiot, eg "STOP F*****G MITHERING"

Moggie

A mouse, definitely not a cat, apparently.

N

Nodders

Condoms - used in the phrase 'lend us one of yer nodders'.

O

Owght

As in 'Any body want owght from't shop ?' - Anything

P

Peas above sticks

You have ideas above your station

Pilks - you weren't born at

You're blocking my view so get out of my way.

Put wood in th' ole

Shut that door

Q

Quaarp

Rather than wasting energy using two syllables, 'Tellins folk turn the Co-operative Society's democratic nomenclature into a noise befitting a parrot

R

Roaghm

As in 'Keep still, stop roaghmin' about' (shouldn't that be 'roaming' about, Ed)

S

Sallywanger

A word of unknown origin, used to scold a young child in days gone by,

Samasase

- 'like I said earlier', or 'like I say...' Word suggested by Mike

Savus

To ask a friend to leave you something after they have
finished with it e.g Savus on that tab, or savus on that butty.

Scon'yed

Scone Head, meaning 'fool'.

Singlet

Meaning vest - i.e. underwear
Submitted by Helen

Skryking

Crying as in - I`ll gi thee summat skryk abarht - submitted by BloorB

Slutchy

Wet and very slippery mud which sticks to
everything that comes into contact with it

Snaped

Unknown origin. Meaning - A bit upset about something.

Sniff - going on the

Making ones focussed vocation to find a soul mate.

Snoo'in

Or possibly 'snowing' - as in "Snoo'in wi twat", meaning "the Plaza was full of nice girls last night".

Split

Chips and Peas with anything i.e Split and Fish, Split and Gravy

Stoney

Alley way, back yard.

Summert

Something

'Tellins word of the day

Clempt

Starvin'.
